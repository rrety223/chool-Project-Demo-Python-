About Us

Since 1978, Riverside High School has served families in Springfield with a commitment to academic excellence and character.

Our mission

We help every student discover their strengths, develop independent thinking and contribute responsibly to their community.

Our values

  • Curiosity - we encourage questions and experimentation.
  • Respect - we value every voice and background.
  • Responsibility - we hold ourselves accountable for our actions.
  • Excellence - we aim high and support each other in reaching our goals.

Campus facts

  • Three science laboratories and a computer lab
  • A 600-seat performing arts auditorium
  • An indoor gymnasium and outdoor sports fields
  • A library with over 30,000 volumes and study spaces
