Admissions

We welcome students from Grade 9 to Grade 12 who are ready to grow with us.

How to apply

  1. Submit an application

    Complete the online form or request a paper copy from the front office.

  2. Attend an interview

    Meet our admissions team and tour the campus with your family.

  3. Take the placement test

    The test helps us place students in the right courses.

  4. Receive your decision

    Decisions are sent by email within 14 days of the interview.

Key dates

  • Open House: October 18, 9:00 AM
  • Application deadline: February 1
  • Placement tests: March 5 and March 12
  • New student orientation: August 25

Tuition and aid

Public high school students attend at no tuition cost. Families who need help with fees, uniforms or transport can apply for support through the front office.
